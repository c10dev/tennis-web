Type, 3D, motion

Type
Match point!
Lilita One for banners, card names and big numbers; Nunito 600/800 for text; Barlow Condensed stays in the score bug.
3D
Toon shading (3-band gradient), ink outlines on players, net and ball only, chunkier proportions, daylight, oversized optic ball with a white trail.
Motion
Springy. Cards deal in with a 70 ms stagger and slight overshoot. The reveal holds 650 ms, both cards flip together, the verdict pops. Count-ups tick upward.
Risk
Costs the most to build: material swap, outline meshes, a rig in this proportion. Can tip into childish if the copy stays jokey. Lilita One has no Hangul.

Type, 3D, motion

Type
Match point
Archivo (variable width: wide for headings, narrow for numbers) with JetBrains Mono for stats and tells.
3D
Flat-shaded low-poly, muted surround, top-down tactical camera at decision points with chalk arrows drawn on the court.
Motion
Drawn-on strokes (arrows and lines draw in over 500 ms), linear flips, little bounce. Count-ups as tally marks.
Risk
Feels like homework at exactly the moments the playtest said were flat (reveal, rewards). Arrow overlays need a new camera mode.

Recommendation: Rally Pop, built in phases

  • Casual: it looks like a game you play in two minutes, not a manager you study. That matches the brief and the playtest.
  • Readable tells: big icon + colour + key on every card, a colour-blind-safe trio, and the opponent's card history as chunky pips.
  • Rewards that land: springy motion, gold for points, optic for "up". It is built for count-ups and overtakes.
  • 1–2 minute loop: nothing to read twice. The banner, three cards and one verdict carry the decision.
  • Cost: phase 1 is CSS tokens, fonts, icons and motion (after the M6 streams merge). Phase 2 swaps renderer materials to toon and adds outlines. Phase 3 picks the rig proportions (TD-012).

Fonts: Barlow, Barlow Condensed, Lilita One, Nunito, Archivo, JetBrains Mono (all SIL OFL 1.1, from Google Fonts). Icons drawn for this page. Court sketches are original. Contrast ratios computed with the WCAG 2 formula; the colour-blind check uses Machado et al. (2009) simulation matrices.
